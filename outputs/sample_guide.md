# Aurelia Bay: your 2-day local mini-guide

A practical starting point based on your interests: food, history.

## 1. Old Lantern Quarter — Lantern Lane
Wander a compact district of preserved merchant houses, tiled courtyards and small artisan studios. Visit in the morning for softer light and quieter lanes.
**Why it fits:** You like heritage streets and handmade design.
**Local tip:** Ask a studio before photographing makers at work.

## 2. Spice Wharf Breakfast — Harbour Market
Try a tasting walk through family-run stalls serving saffron buns, coconut stew and seasonal fruit. Go early while breakfast counters are busy.
**Why it fits:** You enjoy regional food and lively markets.
**Local tip:** Carry small cash and ask vendors about ingredients if you have dietary needs.

## 3. Aurelia Maritime Museum — Civic Harbour
Explore model boats, navigation tools and short exhibits about the bay's fishing communities. The displays make a good rainy-day stop.
**Why it fits:** You enjoy local history and accessible indoor activities.
**Local tip:** Check the museum's current schedule before visiting.

**Planning note:** This is a fictional demonstration destination. Confirm opening hours, prices, transport and accessibility before travel.
